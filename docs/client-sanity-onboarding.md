# Setting Up Your Content Account

Your website's content — your bio, photos, neighborhood guides, blog posts, listings — is managed through **Sanity**, a content platform you own. This account is yours, not ours: nothing about it depends on us, and you keep full control of it for as long as you use the site.

Before we can start building your site, we need you to complete the steps below. It takes about 5 minutes.

## 1. Create your free account

- Go to **[sanity.io](https://www.sanity.io)** and sign up.
- Use your business email (not a personal one) so the account is clearly tied to your business.
- No credit card required — the free plan comfortably covers a single website.

## 2. Create a new project

- Once logged in, click **Create project**.
- Name it something identifiable, like **"[Your Business Name] Website"**.
- Sanity will automatically set up a default dataset called `production` — you don't need to do anything else here.

## 3. Invite your developer

- Inside your new project, go to **Members** → **Invite**.
- Enter your developer's email address.
- Set their role to **Administrator**. This lets them configure the site during setup (they'll need this to build the content structure and connect it to your site). You can lower this to **Editor** — or remove access entirely — once the site is live, if you'd prefer.

## 4. Send your developer two things

Both are visible on your project's **Overview** page and are safe to share over email — they aren't secrets:

- **Project ID**
- **Dataset name** (this will be `production`)

## 5. Wait for your site to be built

Your developer will use these details to connect your new site to your Sanity account.

## 6. Log in and add your content

Once your site is ready, you'll be able to manage all your content at **yoursite.com/studio**, using the same login you created in Step 1.

---

**A note on ownership:** because you created this account yourself, there's nothing to "hand over" later. You own it from day one, and you can revoke anyone's access — including ours — at any time from the Members page.
